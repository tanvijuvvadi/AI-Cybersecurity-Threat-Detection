Detect cyber attacks in network traffic before they cause damage

An end-to-end AI system that learns what normal traffic looks like, spots DoS, brute-force and port-scan attacks (plus unknown anomalies), and raises severity-ranked SOC-style alerts.

The problem

Static rules can't keep up

Signature-based security tools only catch attacks they already know, and they flood analysts with false alarms.

🕳️

Missed new attacks

Evolving and low-and-slow attacks slip past fixed rules.

🔔

Alert fatigue

Too many low-quality alerts hide the real threats.

📈

Scale

Millions of network events per day are impossible to review by hand.

🧠

ML approach

Learn normal behaviour, score every flow, and rank alerts by severity.

Virtual simulation

Simulated cyber attacks

No real corporate logs needed. Each row is one network flow with attack signatures modelled on real behaviour. 35% of attacks are stealthy and blend with normal traffic.

✅

Normal · 80%

Moderate request rates, 0–2 failed logins, few ports.

💥

DoS · 8%

Flood of requests with a high SYN ratio and hundreds of connections per minute.

~500 req/sSYN > 0.8
🔑

Brute Force · 6%

Repeated password guessing against SSH/FTP services.

3+ failed loginsssh / ftp
🔍

Port Scan · 6%

Tiny flows probing many destination ports to map the network.

20–100+ ports
